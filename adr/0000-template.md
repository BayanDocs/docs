# ADR-NNNN: Title in imperative or noun form

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Accepted — validation gate (WP-ID) | Superseded by ADR-NNNN
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders:** who decided (planner session, owner)
- **Related:** requirement IDs, other ADRs, specs, work packages

## Context

What problem are we solving? What forces are at play (requirements, constraints, risks, facts about the ecosystem, with dates and sources)?

## Decision

What we will do, stated precisely enough that an agent can follow it without interpretation.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, what new obligations we take on.

## Alternatives considered

Each serious alternative and why it was not chosen.

## Validation gate (if any)

The work package that must confirm this decision, and the measurable criteria. What happens if they fail.

## Revisit when

Observable triggers that should cause a planning session to reconsider this decision.
